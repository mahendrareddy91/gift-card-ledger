Ledger Packer

Encrypts your cards with a passphrase, on this machine, before they ever go on a server.

Run this locally. Open this file from your own disk (file://). It makes no network requests — your passphrase and card data never leave this page. Nothing here is sent to Claude, Anthropic, or anyone else.

1 · Your ledger

Use the gift-card-ledger-plaintext.json file that came with this packer, or any JSON exported from the app.

2 · Your passphrase

Use a passphrase of several words. There is no recovery — if you lose it, the encrypted ledger cannot be opened by anyone, including you. Keep the plaintext JSON somewhere safe as your backup.

Done. payload.js has downloaded. Put it in the same folder as index.html, then deploy that folder.

3 · Deploy

  1. Put payload.js next to index.html.
  2. Drag the whole folder onto a static host — Netlify Drop, Cloudflare Pages or Vercel all take a folder and give you an HTTPS URL. HTTPS is required: without it the offline service worker will not run.
  3. Open the URL on your iPhone in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open it once while online so it caches, then it works with no signal.
